3

GOOD HEALTH AND WELL-BEING

Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages.

Sustainable Development Goal 3 is one of the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals. It aims to improve health, prevent diseases, and make quality healthcare accessible to everyone.

What is SDG 3?

SDG 3 focuses on ensuring that people can live healthy lives and enjoy physical and mental well-being. It promotes access to healthcare, disease prevention, proper health education, and better health services for communities.

Good health is important because it allows people to learn, work, participate in society, and live better lives.

Key Areas of SDG 3

🤰

Maternal and Child Health

Improve healthcare for mothers, newborns, and children to help prevent avoidable deaths.

🩺

Disease Prevention

Prevent and control communicable and non-communicable diseases through awareness, prevention, and treatment.

🧠

Mental Health

Promote mental well-being and support people experiencing mental health challenges.

🏥

Access to Healthcare

Make quality health services, essential medicines, and vaccines accessible to all.

How HealthTruth Supports SDG 3

HealthTruth supports SDG 3 by sharing understandable health information and helping visitors recognize common health myths.

  • Provides health myths and facts.
  • Encourages visitors to check reliable sources.
  • Promotes awareness of different health topics.
  • Supports informed decisions about health.
  • Encourages healthy habits and disease prevention.

Through health education and awareness, HealthTruth encourages people to become more informed about their health and well-being.

How You Can Help

Stay Informed

Learn about health topics and check trustworthy sources before sharing health information.

Practice Healthy Habits

Maintain good hygiene, eat nutritious foods, stay active, and get enough rest.

Spread Awareness

Share reliable health information and help correct misleading health myths.

4

QUALITY EDUCATION

Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all.

SDG 4 aims to provide everyone with access to quality education, equal learning opportunities, and the knowledge and skills needed throughout life.

What is SDG 4?

Sustainable Development Goal 4 focuses on making education accessible, inclusive, and equitable for everyone. It promotes quality learning, literacy, equal opportunities, and lifelong education.

Education helps people gain knowledge, develop skills, and understand important issues that affect their communities and the world.

Key Areas of SDG 4

📚

Quality Learning

Provide effective learning opportunities and quality education for all learners.

🏫

Equal Access

Ensure that everyone has fair opportunities to attend school and receive an education.

📖

Literacy and Skills

Improve reading, writing, numeracy, and other useful skills for everyday life.

🌱

Lifelong Learning

Encourage people to continue learning and developing knowledge throughout life.

How HealthTruth Supports SDG 4

HealthTruth supports quality education by providing health-related information that visitors can use to learn about health and well-being.

  • Provides educational content about health topics.
  • Explains common health myths and facts.
  • Encourages visitors to check reliable sources.
  • Promotes health literacy and lifelong learning.
  • Helps visitors understand health information.

By making health information easier to understand, HealthTruth contributes to learning and awareness about healthy living.
