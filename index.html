Featured Health Myths

Myth:

Cold weather causes the flu.

Fact:

Influenza, commonly known as the flu, is caused by influenza viruses, not cold weather. Although flu cases are more common during colder seasons in some countries, low temperatures do not directly cause the infection. The virus spreads from person to person through respiratory droplets when an infected individual coughs, sneezes, or talks. Therefore, practicing proper hygiene, washing hands regularly, and getting an annual flu vaccination are important ways to prevent the spread of influenza.

Myth:

Diabetes is caused only by eating sugar.

Fact:

Diabetes is not caused only by eating sugar. It is a condition that develops due to several factors, including genetics, family history, lifestyle, body weight, and the body's ability to produce or use insulin properly. Although consuming too many sugary foods and drinks can contribute to weight gain and increase the risk of developing Type 2 diabetes, sugar alone is not the direct cause. Maintaining a balanced diet, exercising regularly, and having routine health checkups can help reduce the risk of developing diabetes.
